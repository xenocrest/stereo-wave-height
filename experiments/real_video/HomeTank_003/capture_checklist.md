# HomeTank_003 capture checklist

1. Use a standard alternating black/white checkerboard.
2. Verify 9 x 6 internal corners (10 x 7 physical squares).
3. Verify every square is 20.0 mm.
4. Keep the board rigid and flat.
5. Fix both cameras; do not move the rig afterward.
6. Record or photograph a short preflight sample first.
7. Require 54/54 detection and PASS from both cameras.
8. Only then record the full calibration video.
9. Hold each pose for about 1--2 seconds.
10. Capture at least 20 visibly different shared poses.
11. Analyze calibration before any static/wave capture.
12. Record static/wave only after calibration PASS.
