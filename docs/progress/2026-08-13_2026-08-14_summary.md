The planned pre-purchase ideal-simulation campaign is complete. The results
support proceeding to equipment selection and real-system validation, but they
do not select a globally optimal camera geometry or prove real-water accuracy.

## What has and has not been proved

The work proves that the frozen virtual-camera/WASS/grid/evaluation chain can
recover the tested static, regular-wave, irregular-wave, distance, baseline, and
single cross-check cases under ideal synthetic conditions. The comparison
between `(0.20 m,2.50 m)` and `(0.25 m,2.50 m)` is direct evidence that baseline
and working distance are coupled in this pipeline.

It does not prove that 0.25 m is the optimal baseline, that 2.50 m is a generally
valid distance, that constant `B/Z` guarantees success, that the full valid
deployment region has been mapped, or that real cameras and water will achieve
the same accuracy.

## Engineering direction and next work

The delivery target is a locally deployable desktop application rather than a
web page or mini-program. It should guide camera setup and calibration, run the
WASS-based workflow, display reconstruction and quality checks, and export
traceable results locally.

Next work is to freeze a concrete procurement list, purchase and characterize
the stereo cameras/lenses/synchronization/lighting/mounting/computing equipment,
calibrate the real rig, validate static and controlled waves in a water tank,
then establish a new field error budget and deployment envelope. Every stage
must use an independent physical reference and preserve failed runs.

## Repository boundary

This repository contains source, compact metrics, and reviewable documentation.
The complete user-facing DOCX/Markdown report remains local-only. Raw stereo
images, large PNG files, `xyzC`, NetCDF, and WASS run directories must not be
committed.
